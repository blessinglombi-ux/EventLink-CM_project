How Attendance Works

1. Registered The participant registers for your event and receives a free ticket.
2. Scan You scan the QR code on the participant's ticket when they arrive.
3. Present The participant is automatically marked as present.
4. Absent Registered participants who are not checked in can be recorded as absent.
