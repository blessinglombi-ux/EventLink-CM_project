ABOUT EVENTLINK CM

Bringing Events and People Together

EventLink CM is an event discovery and connection platform designed to make it easier for people to discover, organize and participate in events across Cameroon.

🎯 Our Goal

Make event discovery simple and accessible.

🌍 Our Vision

Create a connected event community in Cameroon.

🚀 Our Mission

Help organizers reach attendees and help attendees discover opportunities.
